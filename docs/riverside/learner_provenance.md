# Riverside Evidence Provenance

All Riverside institutions, characters, accounts, and records are synthetic.
No patient records or published dataset rows were used to create them.
Version 2 uses linked records, with a nominal rate of 100,000 new outpatient referral episodes per year across the participating specialty network.
The full case covers January 2025–June 2026, with inherited open records from late 2024.

Your workspace's `evidence.json` identifies the selected release, its cutoff, exact file hashes, and parent manifest hash.
Use that release identity in your analysis notes.
The complete contributor artifact includes generation configuration, source fingerprints, runtime provenance, and model verification evidence.
These are available in the Riverside case repository; they are not required learner conclusions.

The fictional design draws on the following sources:

- Patel et al. (2018), [*Closing the Referral Loop*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5910374/): referral scale and documented outcomes.
- Straus et al. (2011), [*Implementation of an Electronic Referral System for Outpatient Specialty Care*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3243286/): clinical review and communication between practices.
- Suter et al. (2015), [centralized arthritis intake case study](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-015-0033-3): distinct intake tasks and professional responsibilities.
- Odisho et al. (2020), [*Design and development of Referrals Automation*](https://doi.org/10.1093/jamiaopen/ooaa036): incoming documents and electronic referral records.
- Renown Health (2022), [Customer Engagement Center profile](https://www.renown.org/blog/department-spotlight-customer-engagement-center): a central coordination organization.
- Baptist Health's [Paducah overview](https://www.baptisthealth.com/locations/baptist-health-paducah): a hospital's regional outpatient reach.

These sources inform plausibility; they do not establish Riverside's process parameters or prove an explanation for its complaints.
Riverside is not a factual portrait of any of these institutions.
