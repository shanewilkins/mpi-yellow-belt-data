# Riverside Provenance

Riverside v2 is synthetic instructional evidence generated by `model/network.py` from `config/network_v2.yml`.
No patient records or published dataset rows were used to create it.
See the [specification](specification_v2.md) for rules and limitations and the [design record](design_v2.md) for decisions and comparisons.

## Dataset Identity and Reproduction

Version 2.0.0 uses schema `riverside-linked-v2`.
It contains 150,000 new episodes over January 2025–June 2026 and 2,000 inherited open records.
The manifest records exact hashes, row counts, configuration, source fingerprints, the base Git revision, Python version, and lockfile hash.
The base revision alone does not identify working changes; source hashes do.
The [README](README.md) provides generation, verification, and preparation commands.
The tracked release manifest pins the local artifact; bulk data are generated locally and no remote release has been published.

## Sources Informing the Fictional Design

- Patel et al. (2018), [*Closing the Referral Loop: an Analysis of Primary Care Referrals to Specialists in a Large Health System*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5910374/): referral-volume scale, orders versus scheduling attempts, and documented completion. Originating referrals differ from Riverside's receiving-network population.
- Straus et al. (2011), [*Implementation of an Electronic Referral System for Outpatient Specialty Care*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3243286/): specialist review, communication before scheduling, and redistribution of work.
- Suter et al. (2015), [*Optimizing the interprofessional workforce for centralized intake of patients with osteoarthritis and rheumatoid disease: case study*](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-015-0033-3): intake tasks, clinical triage, and professional responsibilities in Canadian models.
- Odisho et al. (2020), [*Design and development of Referrals Automation, a SMART on FHIR solution to improve patient access to specialty care*](https://doi.org/10.1093/jamiaopen/ooaa036): faxed information, electronic records, and staff verification.
- Liedholm (2022), [*Department Spotlight: Customer Engagement Center*](https://www.renown.org/blog/department-spotlight-customer-engagement-center): an institutional account of central coordination, not an independent evaluation; its combined referral-and-authorization workload is not a count of unique referrals.
- Baptist Health's [Paducah profile](https://www.baptisthealth.com/careers/our-locations/paducah) and [hospital overview](https://www.baptisthealth.com/locations/baptist-health-paducah): regional reach, specialties, and outpatient locations beyond the hospital.

Sources consulted during design on 2026-11-05.
They inform plausibility, not Riverside's numerical parameters, causal mechanisms, or pilot effects.
Riverside is not a factual portrait of Baptist Health Paducah or another institution.

## Preserved Version 1

The 1,484-row version-1 dataset, generator, publication aliases, and manifest pin remain intact for existing chapter computations.
Its scenario, dictionary, and provenance are retained under `legacy_v1/` and used by version-1 learner preparation.
Do not apply the version-2 dictionary to the version-1 single-table dataset.
